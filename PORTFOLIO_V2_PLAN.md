# Portfolio V2 — Design and content plan (not published)

**Objective:** present credible evidence of product thinking and business analysis without inflating job titles, conflating personal results with team totals, or presenting unlaunched products as launched.

## Sections to add only after content review

1. **Selected work:** a grid of three focused project cards, each with a factual status badge (concept / prototype / in development / live), one-line problem, audience and role. Candidates: crypto airdrop/points aggregator (hero project), MATHLAB student practice app, Memoir creative journal. Do not assume deployment status.
2. **Case studies:** separate pages with Problem → Evidence & Research → Constraints → Tradeoffs → Decision → Execution → Outcome → Reflection. Distinguish real outcomes from hypotheses.
3. **Experience:** an accurate account of Technical Support Engineer work in fintech (investigation, data, risk, cross-functional delivery), with personal-vs-team metrics explicitly labeled.
4. **Method:** observe → understand → improve, with one deliberate scroll interaction connected to the story, not decorative animation overload.
5. **Resume + contact:** optional downloadable résumé after approval; current email and social links remain prominent.

## Motion and technology

Retain lightweight static HTML/CSS/JS until project-specific interactions materially justify a framework. Avoid a large 3D scene; if a 3D cube interaction is used, defer it until selected work navigation is defined, and provide reduced-motion, touch and keyboard fallbacks.

## Decisions still required

- Which project has a public demo and is safe to show?
- What was personally delivered versus contributed collaboratively?
- Which metrics can be publicly disclosed?
- Do we include a downloadable résumé?

**Do not publish placeholder project cards or fabricated performance claims.**
