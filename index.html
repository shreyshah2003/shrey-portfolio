01 / Separate the report from the cause

“It hasn’t arrived.”
That’s the observation.

A missing transfer is what the person experiences. A broken system is one possible explanation. Mixing the two narrows an investigation before it begins.

Known: the customer reports a delay.
Unknown: status, timing and cause.
02 / Bring the missing context into view

A better question
changes the picture.

What was sent, when, and through which route? What does the available status establish? Compare the report with relevant records, then consider the customer impact, urgency and risk before choosing the next action.

Look for corroboration.
Keep hypotheses separate from facts.
03 / Turn evidence into a useful next step

The answer should
move something forward.

Explain what is known, what remains uncertain and who can resolve it. A good escalation carries evidence and a specific question, so the next team can act.

A clear handoff:
Context → Evidence → Open question → Owner
